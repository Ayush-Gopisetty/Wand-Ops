Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Wand Ops is a free browser game made by Blue Wizard Digital. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it to the minimum the game needs to work.

If you play as a guest

You don't need an account to play. As a guest, your wizard name, chosen skin, coins, owned skins, daily quest progress and game settings are saved only in your own browser's local storage. We never receive them, and clearing your browser data removes them.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. When you sign in we receive a unique account ID and your email address from Google. Your email is only shown to you in the game's top bar; we don't store it in our database or show it to other players.

Under your account ID we store, in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore:

  • Lifetime stats: kills, deaths, assists, damage dealt and taken, spells cast
  • Coins, the skins you own and the skin you have selected
  • Daily quest progress
  • When the record was last updated

During a match

Multiplayer runs through Photon, a real-time networking service by Exit Games. While you're in a match, your wizard name, skin, position, spells, hits, score and any chat messages you send are relayed to the other players in the same match so the game can work. We don't store match data or chat messages. Photon processes connection information, such as your IP address, to relay this traffic.

Anything you type into chat is visible to everyone in your match, so please don't share personal information there.

Analytics and hosting

The game is hosted on Vercel. We use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to count page visits and measure loading performance. These tools collect anonymous, aggregated data and do not use cookies. Like any web host, Vercel processes request information such as IP addresses to deliver the site.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't use advertising or ad tracking.
  • We don't use the information for anything other than running the game.

Children

Wand Ops is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has signed in, contact us and we'll delete their data.

Your choices

  • Play as a guest to keep everything on your own device.
  • Sign out at any time from the top bar.
  • Ask us to delete your account data, and we'll remove your stored record.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and its effective date.
